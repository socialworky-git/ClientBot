Research participation

Help improve how clinicians learn Motivational Interviewing. Socialworky's practice tools are built and maintained by Nathan Kratz, LCSW. With your permission, de-identified records of your practice sessions may be used for research and for publications about how people learn MI skills.

If you opt in:

  • The scores, behavior codes, timing, completion, language, and scenario from each session are kept, as they are for everyone with practice history on.
  • In addition, the text you type and the AI client's and coach's replies are stored, so that the quality of reflections and conversations can be studied.
  • You may answer a few optional profile questions below. Skip any you prefer not to answer.
  • Research datasets replace your account with a random identifier. Your name and email are never part of them.

Participation is voluntary and does not affect your access to any tool. You can withdraw here at any time; that stops further collection, and you can email kratz9@gmail.com to ask that previously collected records be removed. Please never enter real client information into any tool, whether or not you participate.
